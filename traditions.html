Festivals & Traditions

Celebrations that bring communities together.

India's festivals reflect religious traditions, seasonal cycles, regional histories and community life. Their practices vary, while many emphasize family, generosity and togetherness.

Diwali

Festival of Lights

Associated with light, renewal and celebration, Diwali is observed in diverse ways across communities and regions.

Holi

Festival of Colors

Holi is widely associated with color, seasonal change, music, social gatherings and festive foods.

Navratri

Nine nights of celebration

Navratri is observed in different regional forms, with devotional practices, music, dance and community gatherings.

Eid

Community & sharing

Eid celebrations in India include prayer, family gatherings, hospitality, traditional foods and acts of sharing.

Christmas

A celebration across India

Christmas is celebrated by Christian communities and many others, with church services, decorations, music, food and family gatherings.

Regional festivals

Onam, Pongal & Baisakhi

Regional celebrations such as Onam, Pongal and Baisakhi connect community life with harvests, seasons, food and local traditions.

Festivals are not only dates on a calendar; they are occasions through which food, stories, music, rituals and relationships are passed from one generation to another.
