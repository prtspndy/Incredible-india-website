Culture

Traditions that continue to evolve.

Indian culture is expressed through language, dress, music, dance, crafts, customs and the everyday practices of communities across the country.

Festival

Festivals as living cultural traditions

Festivals create spaces for family, community, food, music, prayer, art and shared memory. Their forms differ by region and community, making India's festive calendar especially varied.

Cultural snapshot

Many expressions, many regions.

  • Languages: Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and many other languages and regional varieties.
  • Attire: Saree, kurta-pajama, lehenga, dhoti and numerous regional styles.
  • Dance: Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Odissi and other classical and folk traditions.
  • Crafts: Textiles, embroidery, pottery, painting, metalwork and woodcraft.
Attire

Textiles & traditional dress

Clothing can communicate regional identity through fabric, weaving, embroidery, patterns, colors and methods of draping.

Performing Arts

Dance, music & storytelling

Indian performance traditions combine rhythm, gesture, expression, music and narrative. Classical and folk forms each have distinctive histories.
